# Gift Cards — what ships today

Transcribed from `references/102226/giftCards/`. Selling a gift card is a Pro
Shop category like any other: tap **Gift Card**, fill one screen, SAVE, and the
card lands in the order as a single line to pay for.

**[End to end](?path=/story/flows-gift-cards-current-state-end-to-end--sell-a-purchased-gift-card)**
runs the whole path in one component. **Screens** holds each step on its own.

## The path

| # | Screen | What happens |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Pro Shop | A **Gift Card** tile in the grid |
| 2 | Create a Gift Card | One canvas: amount, UPC, type, expiry, two customers, four categories |
| 3 | Create a Gift Card | A phone search attaches the buyer |
| 4 | Create a Gift Card | A name search offers six Westons; the TO block turns red |
| 5 | Dialog | SAVE explains that the server will generate a UPC |
| 6 | Payment | The ordinary tender screen, $200.00 |
| 7 | Order complete | The receipt |

## What is wrong with it

1. **The biggest control on the screen has no label.** The amount is a white
   box with no caption, no placeholder and no currency symbol. You learn what
   it is by tapping it and watching a number keypad come up.
2. **The expiry is already decided and nobody can read it.** `AUGUST 19 2122`
   on Oct 21, `AUGUST 20 2122` on Oct 22 — so it is **computed from today**,
   roughly ninety-six years out, by a formula nothing on screen explains. It is
   filled in before the operator has entered an amount.
3. **Eight search fields, none marked required**, and the TO block shows **red
   error glyphs on Email and Phone while the search that will fill them is
   still open**.
4. **The search results are the database, unedited.** Four Weston Farnsworth
   records differing only by a `+tag`; `Fake Name`, `buster douglas` and
   `BLOCK Tournament` sharing one phone number. Nothing — no member number, no
   last visit, no balance — tells them apart, and a card issued to the wrong
   one is money moved to a stranger.
5. **A bare `(tr456)` heads the result sheet** and appears nowhere else.
6. **Four spend categories are ticked by default and never explained.**
7. **`Same as From` is a dark button** where a secondary action goes, doing a
   copy rather than a navigation.
8. **SAVE is live from the first frame.** No amount, no buyer, no recipient.
   The only gate is a dialog about a field the screen calls optional — and it
   arrives *after* all the work, with **CANCEL in red and OK in green**.
9. **The order line says `Gift Card` and nothing else.** No recipient, no
   expiry, no code. SubTotal and Grand Total both read $200.00 — a gift card
   is not taxed.

Every one of those is reproduced here rather than corrected.

## The type dropdown is the real story

Captured Oct 22, it holds **three** entries:

| Type | What it is |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Purchased Gift Card** | Somebody pays for it |
| **Customer Credit** | The course owes somebody money and puts it on their account |
| **Winnings** | Somebody won a prize |

Only the first is a sale. The other two are a **ledger adjustment wearing a
till screen's clothes** — same amount box, same two customer searches, same
four spend categories, same green SAVE. Nothing on screen distinguishes selling
someone $200 from granting them $200, and the control that decides which is a
line of plain text halfway down the canvas, unlabelled until it is opened.

Two things fall out of that, and both are worth settling before this screen is
redesigned:

1. **`Gift Card FROM` makes no sense for two of the three.** Winnings are not
   *from* a customer; they are from the course. The screen asks anyway, and
   marks the block's fields in error until something is picked.
2. **A granted balance is money leaving the business with no tender behind
   it.** On this screen it needs the same two taps as a sale, no second
   confirmation, and no note field explaining why.

**What is still a reading rather than a record:** the Oct 22 capture shows the
three names, not what each does to the order. Credit and Winnings are modelled
here as landing at $0.00 and settling with no tender. See
**[7 — The three types](?path=/story/flows-gift-cards-current-state-screens--types)**
and the second and third end-to-end runs.
