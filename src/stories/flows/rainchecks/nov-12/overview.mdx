# What changed — Nov 12

**Two changes, and both of them close something.** Nov 11 asked a question and
drew two answers without choosing. Nov 12 chose, and a second change came back
from the build in the same conversation.

Everything else on the issue screen is as Aug 31 settled it: one hole count for
the group, rows taken off it by hand, the `SET BY HAND` flag, the cap rule, the
reassignment dropdown, voiding, the confirmation synopsis. None of it is
reopened here. **The entire diff is which boxes open ticked, and what shape the
hole control is.**

---

## 1. Option B is chosen

The screen opens with **only the player whose RAINCHECK button was pressed**
ticked.

Weston, looking at the Nov 11 comparison on screen: *"Yeah. This is the one…
that's the one we go [with]."*

So Nov 11 stops being an open question. **Option A — nobody ticked — is
retired**, the same way Aug 20's Option A went once the hole-count decision was
made. It stays in `Flows → Rainchecks → Nov 11` as the record of how the choice
was made, and that page should be read as history from here, not as a decision
waiting on anyone.

What it fixes is still the thing Nevo brought back:

> *"Their main concern is that they don't notice the other ones are checked and
> they accidentally just issued four rain checks. They'd rather have to know
> they're doing it."* — Ryan, Nov 11

**The risk it carries, unchanged and still worth watching:** the tap now means
something it did not mean before. An operator who presses whichever RAINCHECK
button is nearest has chosen that player. The confirmation synopsis is what
stands behind that, and `Select all` is still in the header for the genuine
rained-off foursome.

---

## 2. The slider is replaced by buttons

One key per hole, **0 to 17**, where the group slider was.

This one did not come from the design side. It came back from the build:

> *"We did change the slider [to] the buttons. I forgot to tell you that… Cody,
> he tried the slider, and in the way it's built with the stuff now, he's like,
> oh, that's just not gonna work."* — Weston, Nov 12

It is worth saying plainly that it is **not a concession**:

| | **Slider (Aug 31)** | **Buttons (Nov 12)** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Getting to 12** | A drag that lands near it; read the number back to check | One tap, exactly |
| **Seeing the range** | Two end captions; the middle is discovered | Every answer on screen at once |
| **Width** | Narrow | A full row |
| **Repeated per row** | Four imprecise drags — rejected Aug 20 | Four walls of keys — also rejected |

The output of this control is money. A value that is **chosen** beats a value
that is approximated and then verified, and the cost is width on a landscape
tablet that has it.

The width is also exactly why this is the **group** control only. The rows keep
the stepper. Eighteen keys once is a legible row; eighteen keys four times is a
wall, which is the Aug 20 argument against four sliders arriving intact on the
other side of the swap.

**The end captions stay.** `0 — never teed off` and `17 — all but the last` were
the slider's only statement of its range. Under the buttons they do a different
job: they say what the first and last key *mean*, which bare numbers do not. A
`0` on a row of numbers is ambiguous in a way *"never teed off"* is not — and
`0` is the one value that hands back every dollar.

## What this cost in code

Almost nothing, which is the point of having drawn it rather than estimated it.

- `HoleButtons` in `group-issue.tsx`, beside the stepper it does not replace.
- `holeControl` threaded through `GroupIssueOneStop`, `GroupIssueScreen` and
  `GroupIssueFlow`, **defaulting to `slider`** — so every Aug 31 and Nov 11
  story renders exactly as it did and stays the record of what was decided
  then.
- The row caption reads **with the group** under the buttons, matching the
  build, where the slider version says *same as the group*.
- Option B needed no new code at all. Nov 11 already built `preselect`; these
  stories just pass it.

## One open question for the build

The screenshot from the build spells it **rain check**, two words — in the
title and on the action bar. This design system has said **raincheck**, one
word, since the first Aug 20 screen: story titles, the record, the tender, the
ticket names.

Not changed here, because it is a system-wide rename rather than a screen edit
and it would touch every raincheck story in the library. **Worth deciding
once**, and then doing in one pass, rather than letting the comps and the build
drift apart a word at a time.
