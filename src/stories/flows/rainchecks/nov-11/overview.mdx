# What changed — Nov 11

**One change, and it is a reversal.** The Aug 31 issue screen opens with every
player ticked. Nevo has been running it, likes it, and asked for that one thing
back.

Nothing here reopens the rest of the screen. The hole count shared across the
group, the exceptions coming off it by hand, the reassignment dropdown, the cap
rule, voiding — all settled, all untouched. **The entire change is which boxes
are already ticked when the screen opens.**

---

## Why

> *"Right now, we default to issuing a rain check to all four players. Like,
> they're all checked… They request — normally they're kind of doing it player
> by player."* — Weston

> *"Their main concern is that they don't notice the other ones are checked and
> they accidentally just issued four rain checks. They'd rather have to know
> they're doing it."* — Ryan

The failure is not that the screen is hard to read. It is that an operator who
wanted one credit was handed four, already agreed to, and had to notice and
undo three. The screen proposed it; they only failed to refuse it.

---

## The two options, neither chosen on the call

Both were proposed in the same minute and the call moved on. They are drawn
rather than decided, and the decision is **yours and Weston's** — not something
to settle by picking the tidier diff.

| | **Option A** | **Option B** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Opens with** | Nobody ticked | Only the player whose RAINCHECK you pressed |
| **Whose** | Weston | Ryan — *"either none or only default the player that you clicked"* |
| **Action bar reads** | Nothing selected | Issue 1 raincheck · $39.78 |
| **Rained-off foursome costs** | 4 taps, or one on *Select all* | 3 taps, or one on *Select all* |
| **Single walk-in costs** | 1 tap | 0 taps |
| **The risk it carries** | *Select all* quietly becomes the habit, and the problem moves one tap away | The tap now means something it did not mean before — press the nearest button and you have chosen that player |

Open **[1 — The opening default](?path=/story/flows-rainchecks-nov-11-1-the-opening-default--side-by-side)**
and read the bottom bar in each column. Today's says **Issue 4 rainchecks ·
$206.18** when the operator meant one, which is the complaint in the place it
actually bites.

Then walk both trips. The difference only becomes real once you have pressed
the button that causes it — and Option B in particular cannot be judged from a
still, because the whole of it is *which* button you pressed.

---

## What stays either way

**The confirmation.** Weston's note on the safety net already in place:

> *"Before you finish and issue the rain check, it'll give you a synopsis of
> everything that you're doing… So it's not the easiest thing in the world to
> issue an unintended rain check."*

It stays. But it is a second line of defence, and the argument of this page is
that a default needing one is the wrong default.

**Select all.** Still in the header, where it has always been. Built to clear a
list that arrived full; under either option it becomes the fast path for the
genuine foursome. Worth watching in use — if Nevo presses it every time, Option
A has moved the problem rather than solved it.

**A credited round is never ticked.** In any of the three modes. `includeFor()`
checks that first, ahead of the preference, because a round cannot be credited
twice. There is a story for it.

---

## The case against, kept on the page

Aug 31 chose *everyone ticked* deliberately, and the reason is still in the
source: singling one player out *"would only invite the operator to think the
others are somebody else's problem."* A rained-off foursome really is four
credits, and a default of none makes the common case four taps instead of
nought.

Nov 11 is evidence against that bet, not an argument against it. Ryan, who runs
the courses:

> *"It's just more situations where one person's coming in at a time than all
> four."*

And the reason the solitary case is not an edge case:

> *"We'll give out rain checks for anything, really. Like, someone could hurt
> their back out there. It's not even raining."*

A raincheck is not a weather instrument. That is probably the most useful single
sentence from the call for anyone designing this screen.

---

## What this cost in code

Almost nothing, which is itself an argument for deciding it from screens rather
than from estimates.

- `PreselectMode` and `includeFor()` in `group-issue.tsx` — the one place the
  three modes differ.
- `preselect` threaded through `GroupIssueScreen` and `GroupIssueFlow`,
  **defaulting to `all`**, so every Aug 31 story renders exactly as it did and
  stays the record of what was decided then.
- The tapped player carried from the tee sheet. `onAction` has handed over the
  player all along — it was being dropped on purpose.

## Also from this call, not built here

**The rain check report cannot find a rain check by its ID.** Ryan searched
`49739` in `Reports → Credits → Rain Checks` and got *No rain checks found*;
searching the customer's name finds the same record. Weston reproduced it live.

That is **Buck, not Birdie**, and it reads as a bug rather than a design
question — the search appears to match the name column and not the ID column.
Recorded here so it is not lost with the meeting; it needs an owner either way.

A second, separate effect in the same report: searching before the all-courses
data has finished loading returns nothing, and the search then appears broken
when it is merely early. Worth confirming whether these are one problem or two
before anyone goes looking.
