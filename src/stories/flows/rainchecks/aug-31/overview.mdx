# Aug 31 — the chosen solution

**Option B is the primary solution.** Aug 24 put three options on the table; the
stakeholder has picked one, and everything in this folder is branched from it.
A and C are no longer alternatives.

> **AVAILABLE** — what ships today, and never slower.
> **HISTORY** — everything else, one tap away, with a count badge.

`Aug 24` stays where it is as the record of how the decision was made. **This
folder is what gets built.**

## Option B did not ship as drawn — two holes had to be closed

A chosen solution has to have its known weaknesses fixed before anyone builds
it. B had two, and one of them was written down on the day it was drawn.

### 1. The badge was the only thing preventing a repeat of the incident

Aug 24's own risk note:

> *"The risk is the badge. It is the only thing standing between this and the
> screen that caused the incident. An operator who does not notice it says 'I
> don't see it here' exactly as before."*

A 22px amber pill is not a load-bearing structure. So **the Available tab is not
permitted to render as an empty pane.** When nothing is spendable it shows:

1. **The sentence, meant to be read out loud** — *"3 rainchecks on file. The most
   recent was spent 5/02/2026 at Falls Road — Twilight green fee."* That ends the
   conversation; *"nothing found"* starts a longer one and a trip to a manager.
2. **A 52dp hand-off naming the count**, which moves to History and carries any
   typed query with it.

The badge stays as the ambient signal. A blank pane is precisely the moment
ambient stops being enough.

**This closes the incident as Weston told it, and it involves no search at all.**
The tender already opens on the cart customer.

### 2. A credit found under another name could not be spent

Aug 24 rendered every History row read-only, which was safe while that tab only
ever held dead credits. But History search spans **every customer** — a
misspelling, a spouse's booking, a company account — so it can surface a credit
that is perfectly live. Finding it and not being able to tap it is the same dead
end wearing different clothes.

**History rows are selectable whenever the credit is spendable.**

## The performance worry, answered

> *"My worry is if we include all exhausted rainchecks it will slow down the
> search for the more common use case of actually using a raincheck when there is
> one."*

Option B answers this structurally rather than by argument. **Available's query
is byte for byte the one that ships today** — `searchRainchecks()`, spendable
only. It cannot get slower, because nothing was added to it. The broader question
got its own tab instead of being folded into the narrow one.

That is the whole trade, and it costs no query time.

## The fact that had nowhere to live

**The model had no course.** Not on the credit, not on a redemption.

*"Used it at a different course"* is the load-bearing detail of the incident, and
until Aug 24 there was nowhere to record it — so the terminal could say a credit
was empty but never *where* it went. `Raincheck.course` and `Redemption.course`
now exist. Everything else in this folder is presentation; **this part is data,
and it gates the rest.**

## What the two search surfaces each answer

The History tab and the listing are **not** duplicates, and neither replaces the
other.

| | Question | Who asks it | Where |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **HISTORY tab** | *Can this person pay, and if not, why not?* | An operator, mid-sale, ticket open | Inside the tender |
| **The listing** | *Find me this raincheck* | Whoever currently opens Buck | Its own screen, under Gift Cards |
| **The record** | *What does this person have?* | Somebody who already knows who they are | Customer profile — built, see `Record & register → 3` |

## The rule that governs all of it

> *"We can just change that in the existing system and use this as a good
> breeding ground — this is a new flow that we're trying with the old design
> system, but a new flow."*

**No new tokens, no new colours, no new button styles.** Same navy section bars,
same green, same MD2 surfaces, same 48dp touch floor. What changes is the
arrangement, the defaults, and what the screen says out loud.

Nothing in this folder introduces a component. The tab bar is the only new
control, and it is the shipping tender strip's pattern one level down.

## Still open

Three questions, and they are on the Linear issue rather than settled here.

- **Is the register's lookup scoped to the current course today?** If a
  customer's *unspent* credit from another course is also invisible, that is a
  worse bug than the used one — a live credit failing at the wrong terminal — and
  it should lead this work rather than follow it. **Nobody has checked the
  device.** See *4 → The near miss*. This is the one genuine blocker.
- **Should Available auto-switch to History when it is empty?** It does not
  today, deliberately: switching hides the fact that Available *was* empty, which
  is half of what the operator has to tell the customer. One labelled tap keeps
  both facts. Worth putting to a pro shop rather than settling from a desk.
- **Can an expired credit be honoured with a manager override?** History needs to
  know whether `expired` means *dead* or *ask someone*, and it decides whether the
  listing's expired filter is a report or a worklist.
