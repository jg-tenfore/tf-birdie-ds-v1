# Oct 8 — the chosen refund, and refunding twice

**Option A won.** Weston, with Austin and Jarrett:

> *"I like option A the best. Threw it to Austin and Jarrett, and they think the
> same as well."*

This folder is that option with his notes applied, plus the one genuinely new
capability he asked for: **more than one refund against the same order**. The
Oct 6 folder stays beside it as the record of how the choice was made.

## Refunding an order more than once

Birdie cannot do this today. The design rests on one idea: **the order remembers
what it gave back**, and every screen reads from that remainder.

- **A refunded line is shown, not hidden.** It holds its place, greyed, with a
  `Refunded` chip and a dead checkbox. The operator is holding a receipt; a line
  that quietly vanished would read as a bug.
- **A part-refunded line says so** — `1 of 2 refunded` — and its stepper is
  capped at what is left.
- **A tender that has given back everything it took is closed.** Weston's case:
  cash went back in full on the first refund, so the second one can only go to
  the gift card, and the row says *$21.62 left of $25.58* rather than letting an
  operator pick a tender with nothing in it.
- **`Select all items` means all of what is left**, not all of what was sold.
- **An order with nothing left stops offering REFUND**, on the transaction
  screen, before a refund is started. The modal refuses on its own terms too.
- **Each refund writes its own reversal order.** A second refund is a second
  reversal, never an edit of the first — the reporting model Weston wanted kept,
  now applied more than once to one sale.

### The order between refunds

The transaction screen gains three things and loses nothing: a status band
(*Partially refunded · $28.96 returned · $21.62 still on this order*), per-line
chips, and per-tender state in Payments. That screen is what makes a second
refund safe to start, and answers his question directly — *"does it look like
now that these two items have been refunded?"*

## Still open

1. **Tax on a partial refund.** Prorated from the order total, as before. If the
   backend can return tax per line, use that instead — it matters on mixed-rate
   baskets, and it matters more now that one order can be refunded in pieces.
2. **Refunding to a tender that was not on the order** (cash back on a card
   sale). Not drawn; nobody has said whether it should be allowed.
3. **Permissions.** Nothing here asks who is refunding. If a manager PIN is
   wanted, the confirmation is where it belongs — and a second refund on an
   order is the likeliest place to want one.
4. **The reason list.** Four plus `Other`. What `Other` collects in the first
   month should decide what gets promoted into the list.
