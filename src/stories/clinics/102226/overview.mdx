# Oct 22 — one registration, not one booking

[102126](?path=/docs/flows-clinics-102126-overview--docs) asked *how many
players* and sold them all the same dates in one clinic. Weston came back with
the whole job.

## The flow he described

> *"Click on clinics and you'd first be asked how many players. Right off the
> get-go… and then you collect their information. We figure out who they are…
> and then you step through it for each person you're registering for."*

> *"What if I want to do the putting lab and the junior golf academy? I want
> them to do both. So if you could select both — it's more of like a
> multi-select."*

> *"And then maybe we add a 'same as Justin' and it just selects it and skips
> those steps… but then he actually can't do Tuesdays because he has baseball,
> so I'm going to put him on Wednesday."*

> *"Then we could have this confirmation screen — you're registering Justin for
> these, Weston for these, for this amount. Hit add to cart. And maybe there's
> edit buttons."*

So: **several players, each in several clinics, each on their own dates**,
assembled in one pass. All three versions do all of that, and they are
identical until the dates.

## Three other things he settled

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| **The rail comes back** | *"I was wrong. If you do have something in the cart, it will show there in the rail… let's add the rail back then. To keep it consistent."* It is on every screen here, and has been restored in 102126 |
| **It ends at the cart, not the card** | *"Adding it to the cart and not skipping gives us the most flexibility… is there that 10% where the course comes and complains because they needed to add a dozen golf balls and now it's two transactions?"* |
| **Quantity discounts get called out** | *"You can set up a discount… if you buy 4, you get them 25% off. All 4 of them."* → *"On this confirmation screen we may want to just call that out — hey, add one more and get 25% off."* |

## The one question he handed back

> *"And then it would take you to selecting the dates. That's one question, and
> I would leave this open to you, because there could be a list this long. So
> maybe it says, this is for Justin — and then **do we do each clinic by
> itself, or is it like all on this page?**"*

That is the only thing A, B and C disagree about.

| | **A — Clinic by clinic** | **B — One page per player** | **C — Confirm first** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| After the multi-select | A dates page per clinic | Every clinic stacked on one page | Same as B, for the first player only |
| Players 2 onward | Step through the same pages | Step through the same pages | **Rows on the summary** — copy, or open in place |
| Pages for 2 players × 2 clinics | **10** | **6** | **5** |
| Reads well when | Long date lists, one clinic at a time | Two or three clinics each | The second player is mostly the same as the first |
| The cost | Step fatigue | A long scroll, totals below the fold | The summary does two jobs at once |

**A** is his literal description. **B** is the other answer to his question.
**C** takes his own shortcut — *"it just skips those steps"* — and removes the
steps for everybody after the first player.

## Availability is now per player

102126 asked the party size and greyed any date that could not seat all of
them. That rule cannot survive a flow where players take *different* dates, so
it is replaced: **each date's count comes down as players are assigned to it**,
inside this registration, before anyone reaches the till. Put two of a family
on a date with two places and the row reads none left.

It is the Oct 20 complaint, kept honest: *"users can fill out full registration
and only hit the limit at the very end."*

## The cart

**One line per player per date**, named and dated —
`Justin — Junior Golf Academy — 10/7/26 10:30 AM`. A discount earned on a
clinic is spread evenly across that clinic's lines, so every line shows what it
actually cost. The shipping flow drops three lines reading *Come learn the
game!* with no dates and no guest on them.

## About the numbers

The availability counts and the twenty-clinic catalogue carry over from
[102126](?path=/docs/flows-clinics-102126-overview--docs) and are invented
there. The **discounts are invented too** — three clinics carry a rule, at 4 or
5 dates for 15–25% off.

One thing to correct when it matters: the device configures a discount on the
**instance**, not the clinic — *"I think you said it on the clinic level. Oh,
that was the instance."* These versions hang it on the clinic, which is enough
to show the arithmetic and the nudge but is not where it really lives.
