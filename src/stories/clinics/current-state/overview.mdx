# Clinics — what ships today

Transcribed from `references/102026/`. Selling a clinic is a Pro Shop category
like any other: tap **Clinics**, pick a clinic, pick its dates, search for the
guest, SAVE — and the booking lands in the order as ordinary lines to pay for.

**[End to end](?path=/story/flows-clinics-current-state-end-to-end--sell-a-clinic)**
runs the whole path in one component. **Screens** holds each step on its own.

## The path

| # | Screen | What happens |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Pro Shop | A **Clinics** tile in the grid, with no badge or count |
| 2 | Clinic list | One card: name, type, and the number `10` |
| 3 | Session picker | Ten dates as chips, plus a six-field customer search |
| 4 | Session picker | SELECT ALL takes the eight that are not full |
| 5 | Pro Shop order | Three lines land in the cart |
| 6 | Payment | The ordinary tender screen, $74.97 |
| 7 | Order complete | The receipt, and the four ways out |

## What is wrong with it

1. **The `10` is the maximum roster, not availability** — and nothing says
   which. Weston: *"no indication of spots remaining before checkout. Users can
   fill out full registration and only hit the limit at the very end."*
2. **A full session is told apart by a word inside a chip** — `(FULL)` and a
   shade of grey — while the eight bookable ones say nothing about how many
   places they hold.
3. **The order panel is empty on both clinic screens**, and takes a third of
   the canvas with it.
4. **No price appears anywhere** until after SAVE. The first figure of any kind
   arrives on screen 5.
5. **The clinic is named two ways in one sale**: its blurb in the order panel
   (*Come learn the game!*), its name on the receipt.
6. **Three lines appear where eight sessions were chosen**, at $0.00, $22.47 and
   $44.94, with no dates on any of them. Nobody could explain the free line.
7. **The customer search has no required field** and no result list in the
   capture, so which combination of the six attaches a guest is unstated.

Every one of those is reproduced here rather than corrected — with one
exception, noted in `clinic-screens.tsx`: the device's radio overlaps the first
character of a two-line date, and that overlap is fixed so the screen can be
read.

**[102026](?path=/docs/flows-clinics-102026-overview--docs)** is the response to
points 1–4, in three versions.
