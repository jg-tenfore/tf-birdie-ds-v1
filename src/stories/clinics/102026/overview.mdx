# Oct 20 — fixing the clinic screens

Three versions of the two screens Weston named, from his walkthrough of the
shipping flow. **Current state** sits beside this folder as the record of what
ships today.

He reviewed these on Oct 21 and took **B's rows and the stepper**; what he asked
for next is in
[102126](?path=/docs/flows-clinics-102126-overview--docs).

## What he asked for

| His note | What it means on screen |
| :-- | :-- |
| *"The `10` shown on the clinic tile represents max roster size, not availability"* | The number on the card is unlabelled and answers the wrong question |
| *"No indication of spots remaining before checkout. Users can fill out full registration and only hit the limit at the very end"* | Availability has to arrive before the work, not after it |
| *"Show per-instance availability — e.g. 2 spots left per date"* | Every session carries its own count |
| *"Remove the 'no items in order' shelf, not used"* | The empty order panel goes, on both clinic screens |
| *"Clean up within the existing design system"* | Same chrome, same controls, no new visual language |

All three versions do all five. They differ in how far they move.

## The three

| | **A — Counts in place** | **B — Roster first** | **C — Guest first** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| The change | Same screens, every number labelled | Chips become rows | The job is reordered |
| Dates | Chips, each with its own count | A table: date, time, fill meter, count, price | Cards, checked against the guest |
| The guest | The six fields, kept | The six fields, under the dates | One search field, asked first |
| Price | A line under the dates | A summary bar above the action bar | A running rail on the right |
| Best when | The fix should ship this sprint | A manager wants to see how the clinic is filling | The roster check at the end is what is hurting |
| Risk | Lowest | Medium | Highest |

**A** is the small change he described: nothing moves, everything is labelled,
and the freed order-panel space pays for a second line on each chip.

**B** treats a clinic as a roster with dates attached. Ten rows in columns are
read down; ten chips of text are read one at a time, and the nearly-full dates
are the ones that matter.

**C** asks *who* before *when*. Because the guest is known first, a date they
already hold reads **Already booked** and cannot be chosen twice — a check the
current design cannot make, since it never learns the guest until after the
dates are picked.

## Running them

Every customer field in these flows **fills on tap**: the forms are not typable,
so tapping one stands in for the lookup and fills the whole record. Option C
starts its guest step empty, so the run shows the lookup happening rather than a
guest who was always there.

## Two things all three fix that were not on the list

- **Price appears before SAVE.** The shipping screens show no figure anywhere;
  the first number anyone sees is in the order panel on the next screen.
- **A full session loses its radio.** Today it keeps one, which invites a tap
  that does nothing.

## About the numbers

The availability counts are **invented**. The device never exposes a remaining
figure — that is the complaint — so the two sessions it marks `(FULL)` are the
only real ones. The spread (one date with a single place left, two nearly full,
the rest open) is there to show the states, not to describe this clinic.

Still open: what the order line should say once a booking lands in the cart.
Today it prints the clinic's blurb, *"Come learn the game!"*, with no dates and
no guest — recorded under **Current state**, step 5, and untouched here.
