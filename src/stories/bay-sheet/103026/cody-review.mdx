[1 — The day](?path=/story/bay-sheet-oct-30-screens--sheet).

## The two things waiting on an answer

**1. The colours on the ribbon.** Weston named *simulator member* and *public*
out loud, so the set is Simulator Member / Member / Public, and that part is
settled. The **colours** are not: they were picked to stay apart at 4px and to
avoid the green that already means *paid*, not matched to anything. If the tee
sheet's membership bar already has values for these statuses, those should win
— Weston's whole argument for the ribbon was *"that's the pattern we've adopted
on the tee sheet,"* and staff should not have to learn it twice. A screenshot
of that bar is enough. It is a data change in `bay-data.ts`.

The list could also be wrong, if a course has more than three types. Same file,
same shape, no code change.

> **Answered Nov 3 — and the last paragraph was the right worry.** There is no
> fixed set at all. A course creates unlimited memberships and picks each
> one's colour on the plan, and the tee sheet already draws those colours.
> The three invented colours are gone and the ribbon reads the membership
> record. Rebuilt, not patched.

**2. Where a course would set the full-card preference.** Cody raised this
himself — *"obviously the question is, where are they going to modify all these
settings?"* — and nothing here answers it. Deliberately left, because
full-card is a fallback for a complaint nobody has made yet, and the complaint
would also be the brief for where the switch lives.

> **Still open after Nov 3**, and now with a second part to it: with courses
> choosing their own colours, a filled card cannot assume white text. That
> half is solved — the ink flips per chip — but where the preference lives is
> still unanswered.

**Settled since the review, for the record:** the slide-over is 560, and
multi-bay blocking fans out on edit as well as create — ticking extra bays on
an existing block moves that one and creates the rest, because *"also block
these"* is what the extra ticks mean.

## Noted, and deliberately not built yet

**Several schedules in one day.** From the top of the review: today there is
one schedule per day and no way to break a day into parts, so a course cannot
say members book 8–12, the public books 12–4, and members again after 8. Cade
is building that on the backend. It will change how the day is drawn — named
windows down the clock gutter, and probably a rule about which bookings may
land in which window — and that is a design pass of its own rather than a
tweak to these screens. Say when it is close and it gets specced.

**The old/new view toggle.** Cody's idea, and his to implement: a button that
flips a page between the new design and the old one, cached per device until
the next install. Nothing is needed from design for it.

## How this category will work from here

Anything new or revised gets a dated page like this one, and the screens it
touches get linked rather than copied. Items that Justin and Weston have both
signed off say **build it** — that is the mark to look for, and it means no
sync is needed first. Anything either of them wants to talk through says
**needs an answer** and names the question.

The Linear tickets follow the Rain Checks shape, since that worked: one parent
project, smaller child tickets per phase, so phase 1 can ship while phase 2
sits still.
